# CapstoneProject-Logistic-S.p.a

## Project Overview

**Capstone Project - Challenge 1: Logistics SpA - Analysis and Prediction of Business Performance**

This is a master's final project focused on customer segmentation and profiling for **Logistic SpA**, a leading logistics provider in Italy.

## Company Context

**Logistic SpA** (fictitious name of a real company for privacy reasons) is a leader in the logistics industry in Italy and faces fierce competitive pressure from competitors. The organization seeks to leverage data science to better understand and segment its customer base.

## Problem Statement

The company has hired a Data Science team to conduct a comprehensive **customer segmentation and profiling** analysis based on:
- Customer sectors
- Customer size
- Purchasing behavior patterns (recent past)

## Project Goals

### Customer Classification & Categorization

1. **Client Classification by Purchase Behavior**
   - Classify clients as: continuous, recurring, uniform, or variable based on purchasing patterns

2. **Client Status Flagging**
   - Identify and flag: new clients, lost clients, and returning clients

3. **Client Size Categorization**
   - Categorize clients by size according to their revenues (Fatturato) over a defined period

4. **Correlation Analysis**
   - Search for correlations between the above dimensions and other client descriptors:
     - **Size indicators**: Fatturato (Revenue), Addetti (Employees)
     - **Field of operations**: ATECO (Italian economic sector classification)
     - **Legal entity type**: Natura (Legal form)
     - **Geographic location**: Provincia (Province)

## Dataset & Resources

### Data Files
- **dataset.csv** - Main customer and transaction dataset
- **Ateco.xls** - ATECO mapping file for economic sector classification


## Key Deliverables

- Customer segmentation models
- Profiling analysis with demographic and behavioral insights
- Correlation analysis results between client dimensions
- Business recommendations based on findings

## Getting Started

1. Load the dataset.csv and Ateco.xls files
2. Explore data structure and quality
3. Perform customer segmentation analysis
4. Generate profiling reports and visualizations
5. Document findings and business recommendations
